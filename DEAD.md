## Policy

A published v3 report that fails is never removed from the ranking in silence. It stays on RANKING.md until a dated entry is added here with a reason. Unscored pipeline names can move here for official shutdowns, unfixed exploits, wind-downs, or a documented dying-market skip. **Dying-market skip rule:** Market cap under $1M and 24h volume under $10k, as of a dated public market read, and only when an explicit entry is added on this list. This is not applied automatically to every thin name on the All Projects scan.

**Classes used in the 16 Nov 2026 pass:** 16 Nov 2026 pass over the thin-market pipeline names. Dying: 24h volume under $10k on 16 Nov 2026, with the markets and the latest trade or on-chain transfer date given. Probably dead: no active market on CoinGecko or CoinPaprika and near-zero volume, often with the official site down; tagged [Unverified] until an official notice is read. Wound down: an official shutdown, migration or merger notice is linked. A recent on-chain transfer can be dust or spam and does not by itself show a live project. Names with real volume (24h volume of $10k or more on active markets) stay in the pipeline.
