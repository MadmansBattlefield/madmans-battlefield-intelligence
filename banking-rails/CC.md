## 💰 Revenue — Does it make money? · 6/10 · ✅ PASS

**How the money works** ([Tier A] [Canton docs, tokenomics](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/canton-coin-tokenomics); [Tier A] Digital Asset letter to the SEC, 30 Oct 2026): users pay **traffic fees** in CC to send transactions through the Global Synchronizer. Fees are set in US dollars, and **every CC paid is burned**. "No portion of the Canton Coin used to pay network fees is received by DA, the Canton Foundation or any other person." Transfer and lock fees were removed by CIP-0078; only traffic and holding fees remain.

**The catch — part of the burn earns rewards back:**
- Validators "earn minting rights proportional to the fees they burn", and featured apps get roughly **$1 of minting weight per qualifying round** under the legacy marker system (now moving to traffic-based rewards under CIP-0104, default $0.50 per coupon) ([Tier A] Canton docs).
- So some of the burn is paid by parties who then mint new CC for that same activity. How much of the $650M is genuine outside demand vs. reward farming is **[Unverified]** — no public split exists.

**What it means:** This is real, measurable fee burn, and all of it reaches holders as reduced supply rather than going to a company. It is large next to the $3.3B market cap (about 20% a year). It still does not cover new issuance: over 12 months supply grew about 14%, and some burns are recycled into rewards.

### How scores work

- Each section is scored **0–10** from the evidence on the page. **6 or more = ✅ PASS.** Under 6 = ❌ FAIL. "Not proven" counts as a fail.
- **Overall** = weighted average: Revenue ×3, Selling behavior ×2, Holdings ×1.5, Liquidity ×1.5, every other section ×1. Overall PASS needs **6.0 or more**.
- **Hard gates:** if Revenue or Selling behavior scores **3 or below**, the overall verdict is ❌ FAIL regardless of the average.
- **Pump day** = a daily close at least 10% above the close five days earlier. A team, foundation or treasury wallet selling (or funding a wallet that sells) on a pump day, without disclosure, fails Selling behavior.
- Sources: [Tier A] = primary (chain reads, official docs, filings, the DEX's own API). A fact needs 1 Tier A or 2 Tier B, or it is marked [Unverified]. Unknowns say "Not found" and what would answer them.
- Research only. Not investment advice.
