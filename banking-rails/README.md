Research notes on tokens and protocols around digital settlement, payroll, tokenized assets, stablecoins, and the major-market tape that prices them. Part of **Madman's Battlefield Intelligence**.

Not financial advice. Research only. Times are in **MST**.

**Start here:** [Cover sheet](COVER.md) (why this exists, methodology, what we look at) · [Ranking](RANKING.md) (all 28 v3 scores, ranked)

## 📐 Report layout (v3)

Every token in this folder has a full v3 scored report:

1. **YAML front matter** — ticker, scores, gates, red lines, price and source, `layout_version: 3`
2. 🧾 **Quick review card** — overall grade, one-line summary, pass or fail for each of the 11 sections
3. 🪪 **Full report** — market and on-chain snapshot, then every section with its score in the header and all the supporting data
4. 🚩 **Red lines** — token-specific tripwires
5. 📚 **Sources** — each tagged **[Tier A]**, **[Tier B]**, or **[Unverified]**
6. **How scores work** — the v3 weighting and hard gates (also in the [cover sheet](COVER.md))

[other-tokens.md](other-tokens.md) holds shorter, unscored stubs on other names.

**Verification rule:** a fact is verified only with 1 Tier A source or 2 Tier B outlets (see [sources.md](../sources.md)). Everything else is labeled **unverified**.
