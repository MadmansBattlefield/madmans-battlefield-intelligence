## 🧾 QUICK REVIEW CARD

### NEXA · Overall grade **3.8 / 10 · ❌ FAIL**

**Summary:** Nexa is a proof-of-work chain from the nonprofit Bitcoin Unlimited. It launched in 2022 with no premine, sale or team allocation. All 21T NEX are mined, and fees go to miners, so nothing accrues to holders. Its next upgrades (Tailstorm three-second subblocks, then NexaPoW 2.0) aren't live yet. Trading is mostly on MEXC, and CoinGecko's aggregate volume is only ~$8k a day. NEX is down 99% from its 2023 peak. The revenue gate fails.

**🚨 Top warning:** **liquidity is almost entirely one exchange.** MEXC holds **1.57T NEX (14.0% of supply)** in a wallet the [Nexa explorer rich list](https://explorer.nexa.org/rich-list) labels as MEXC. MEXC's NEXA-USDT pair shows ~$59k a day, but CoinGecko's aggregate volume is only ~$8.4k. Most other venues trade under $4k a day. The project fielded community questions in 2026 after another exchange, Biconomy, suspended deposits and withdrawals ([forum](https://forum.nexa.org/t/answering-community-questions/1697)). Every link is in the 🔗 Evidence trail below.

### How scores work

- Each section is scored **0–10** from the evidence on the page. **6 or more = ✅ PASS.** Under 6 = ❌ FAIL. "Not proven" counts as a fail.
- **Overall** = weighted average: Revenue ×3, Selling behavior ×2, Holdings ×1.5, Liquidity ×1.5, every other section ×1. Overall PASS needs **6.0 or more**.
- **Hard gates:** if Revenue or Selling behavior scores **3 or below**, the overall verdict is ❌ FAIL regardless of the average.
- **Pump day** = a daily close at least 10% above the close five days earlier. A team, foundation or treasury wallet selling (or funding a wallet that sells) on a pump day, without disclosure, fails Selling behavior.
- Sources: [Tier A] = primary (chain reads, official docs, filings, the DEX's own API). A fact needs 1 Tier A or 2 Tier B, or it is marked [Unverified]. Unknowns say "Not found" and what would answer them.
