## 🧾 QUICK REVIEW CARD

### NIGHT · Overall grade **3.8 / 10 · ❌ FAIL**

**Summary:** Midnight is a privacy-focused chain linked to Cardano. Its NIGHT token launched in Dec 2025 through the Glacier Drop. It trades well ($35.9M a day on Binance, OKX and Kraken), but by design holders earn nothing: network fees are paid in DUST, a non-transferable resource generated by holding NIGHT. No protocol revenue was found, so the revenue gate fails. The bigger issue is concentration: **two related entities hold 50.25% of supply, all of it unlocked since day one**, and the Foundation says it "may take decisions … that conflict with the interests" of other holders.

**🚨 Top warning:** half the supply is entity-controlled and free to move. The tokenomics whitepaper states that the **Midnight Foundation holds 8.4B NIGHT (35%)** and **Midnight TGE Ltd. holds 3.66B (15.25%)**, both "unlocked at the start of the Redemption Period on 10 December 2025 and … not subject to any protocol-level lockup". Their multisig thresholds and signers are not disclosed, and publishing material transfers is "a statement of present intent only" ([whitepaper](https://45047878.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/45047878/Midnight-Tokenomics-And-Incentives-Whitepaper.pdf)). Block production is federated: 13 nodes, two of them run by the Foundation, which pays the rest. Every link is in the 🔗 Evidence trail below.

## 💰 Revenue — Does it make money? · 1/10 · ❌ FAIL (hard gate)

- Transaction fees on Midnight are paid in **DUST**, which holding NIGHT generates and which cannot be transferred. NIGHT holders therefore receive no fee income, and there is no burn or treasury capture. No DefiLlama fee or revenue listing exists. **1 → hard-gate FAIL.**

- **Reaches holders (binary test, 16 Nov 2026 rules): No.** Fees are paid in non-transferable DUST; NIGHT holders receive no fee income and there is no burn.

## 🏛️ Foundation & team — Who is in charge? · 5/10 · ❌ FAIL

- The Midnight Foundation runs ecosystem spending and is on the governance committee; Midnight TGE Ltd. handles the token distribution. Both are disclosed entities with written policies, but multisig structures and signers are not disclosed ([whitepaper](https://45047878.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/45047878/Midnight-Tokenomics-And-Incentives-Whitepaper.pdf)).

### How scores work

- Each section is scored **0–10** from the evidence on the page. **6 or more = ✅ PASS.** Under 6 = ❌ FAIL. "Not proven" counts as a fail.
- **Overall** = weighted average: Revenue ×3, Selling behavior ×2, Holdings ×1.5, Liquidity ×1.5, every other section ×1. Overall PASS needs **6.0 or more**.
- **Hard gates:** if Revenue or Selling behavior scores **3 or below**, the overall verdict is ❌ FAIL regardless of the average.
- **Pump day** = a daily close at least 10% above the close five days earlier. A team, foundation or treasury wallet selling (or funding a wallet that sells) on a pump day, without disclosure, fails Selling behavior.
- Sources: [Tier A] = primary (chain reads, official docs, filings, the DEX's own API). A fact needs 1 Tier A or 2 Tier B, or it is marked [Unverified]. Unknowns say "Not found" and what would answer them.
