# 📝 Dual-review reconciliation

**This is a template.** Copy it to a dated note (for example `reconciliation/TICKER-YYYY-MM-DD.md`) when a dual review of Selling or Holdings disagrees by 2 or more points. Do not fill this file in place. No dual reviews were run in the 16 Nov 2026 methodology pass.

Research only. Not financial advice. Times are in **MST**.

The process is defined in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md). Two scorers grade Selling and Holdings independently. A gap of **2 or more points** on a section requires a public note in this form, linked from the token report and recorded in `data/reports/<TICKER>.json` under `review.dual_review`.

## Why they differed

Write the disagreement in plain language. Stick to evidence already in the report or added with a source:

- Which wallets or transactions were in scope?
- Was a label treated as high, medium, or low confidence?
- Was there independent corroboration?
- Was the day a pump day under the pre-registered UTC rule?
- Was a sale disclosed, buried, or met with silence?

Do not introduce a private worksheet, a personal name, or a wallet that is not going to be published in the report.

## Agreed reading
